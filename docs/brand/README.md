# The monokulo-lws mark

A medieval lantern whose glass is a rose window: a lantern for light wallets.
The rose is Monokulo's own lens cut (the curve tree its mark is drawn from:
a hexagon root, six branches, twelve limbs and the tips of twelve leaves),
each facet a pane of stained glass, lightest at the root. The panes sit
edge to edge with no leading between them: the colours alone mark the facets.
The ring on top is Fob's.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `lws-mark.svg` | On light backgrounds, 48 px and up. |
| `lws-mark-on-dark.svg` | On dark backgrounds, 48 px and up: the ironwork is burnt iron, not ink. |
| `lws-mark-small.svg` | 32 px and below (favicons, LuCI) on light backgrounds: the lantern housing (ring, roof, thick frame, base) with a close-up of the rose as its window. |
| `lws-mark-small-on-dark.svg` | The same small mark for dark backgrounds, in burnt iron. |

The small marks show the full mark's lit rose facets at 2.3 times the size,
clipped to the window, so no circle edge shows. They have no hexagon
ornament and no rim: at that size both would only be noise.

All four files use `viewBox="3 -1 94 94"`. The earlier box (`4 1.5 92 92`)
clipped the stroke of the ring on top.

Colours are the family's: `#FF6600`, `#E85D00` and `#C24E00` for the glass,
`#1A1917` ink, `#F4F1EA` paper, and two lighter oranges for the lit panes,
`#FF8A33` and `#FFB066`. On dark backgrounds the ironwork is burnt iron,
`#7E4219`: deep orange `#C24E00` mixed half and half with charcoal `#3A3733`.
